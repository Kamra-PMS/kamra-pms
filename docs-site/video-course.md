# Video course

A free 27-part video course that walks through Kamra from the first sign-in
to night audit, reports and AI agents. Each episode is about 8 minutes,
narrated, with chapters and English captions.

**[Watch the full course on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLcNTRQwzkzAs)**

Episodes are released three a week (Monday, Wednesday and Friday). New
episodes appear in the playlist as they go live. Every episode follows the
demo hotel, so you can [try the live demo](/demo) and repeat the steps
yourself.

Each episode pairs with a written guide. Use the video to see the flow,
then the guide for every field and setting.
