# Banquets & Groups — functions from enquiry to close-out

The Banquets & Groups app runs the function business: weddings,
conferences, launches, parties. One **function sheet** carries a function
from the first call through the quotation, the event order and the money,
to closing the hall on the night. Front Desk and Revenue Manager staff
work on functions; Finance and Housekeeping can read them.

![The Banquets home: confirmed, still in play, outstanding and conversion, with the list of functions that need chasing](/img/banquets/home.png)

## Before you start

1. **Add your halls.** **Banquets & Groups → Halls & Venues → New**. Enter
   the **Venue name**, **Type**, **Seats (maximum pax)**, **Minimum pax
   it's worth opening for**, **Day rental**, **Hourly rental**,
   **Minimum hours**, the rental tax rate and **Layouts it takes**.

   ![The Halls & Venues list: Boardroom, Riverside Deck, Garden Lawn and Grand Ballroom with seats, day rental and hourly rate](/img/banquets/venues.png)

2. **Build the catalogue.** **Banquets & Groups → Menus & Services**. A
   **menu** has a **Rate per pax**, a **Minimum pax** and its courses. A
   **service** is everything else — LED wall, podium, DJ, decor, bar,
   staffing — with a rate and whether it's **Chargeable by default**.
   **Dishes** hold recipes, so the margin can count food cost.

   ![The banquet catalogue: five menu packages with plate price, minimum pax and courses](/img/banquets/catalogue.png)

3. **Check the defaults.** **Banquets & Groups → Settings**: target and
   floor margin, the default payment milestones and how soon to follow up
   an enquiry. See [Settings](#settings).

## Find a free hall

- **Month Availability** shows every hall by session (morning, afternoon,
  evening) for a month. Click an open slot to start an enquiry on it. The
  bar at the top counts the weekend evenings still open.
- **Function Diary** shows each hall for a fortnight. Click a day to check
  it, or a function to open it. **Check a date** looks up one date.

![Month Availability: four halls by morning, afternoon and evening for October, with a tentative booking on two evenings](/img/banquets/month.png)

![The Function Diary: halls down the side, a fortnight across, a function block at the end](/img/banquets/diary.png)

A **confirmed** function owns the hall. A **tentative** hold is a soft
hold: the hall shows as held, but you can still sell over it. Two
functions can share a hall on one day as long as their hours don't
overlap.

## Take an enquiry

1. On **Banquets**, click **New enquiry**.
2. Enter the **Customer name**, **Phone** and **Email**. Pick a
   **Company** for corporate business; it decides which folio the bill
   goes to.
3. Pick the **Event type**, **Date** (and **Ends** for a multi-day
   function), **Expected pax** and **Session**. A session (**Evening**
   and so on) sets the hours and is saved on the function. Choose
   **Custom Hours** to give your own **From** and **To**.
4. Pick where the guest **Came from**.
5. Under **Which hall**, pick a hall. Halls that are taken can't be
   picked; Kamra warns when a hall is smaller than the pax count.
6. Write **What they asked for** in the guest's words.
7. Click **Open the enquiry**.

![A new function enquiry: customer, event type, date, pax, session and the hall picker with Grand Ballroom selected](/img/banquets/new-enquiry.png)

The hall's rental goes on the quote as the first line, and a follow-up
date is set (2 days by default).

![Opening an enquiry, then adding a cocktail menu and an LED wall to its quote](/img/banquets/enquiry-to-quote.gif)

## Work through the function sheet

The sheet has six steps across the top. Each one shows a tick when it's
done, or the one thing still missing. You can open any step at any time.

![The function sheet header: quote, received, balance and margin, and the six steps — Enquiry, Quote, Margin, Money, Documents, The night](/img/banquets/function.png)

| Step | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Enquiry** | Event details, setup style, guaranteed pax, contact, billing and tax details, green room, notes. |
| **Quote** | The lines, discount and service charge. |
| **Margin** | Revenue against cost, and the kitchen indent. |
| **Money** | Payment terms and receipts. |
| **Documents** | Quotation, contract, event order, pack list, invoice. |
| **The night** | Count what was served, damages, deposit, close-out. |

## Build the quote

1. Open the **Quote** step.
2. Click **+ Menu** and **Add** a menu package. Its quantity follows the
   guaranteed pax.
3. Click **+ Service** and **Add** what they're buying. Click **Free**
   instead to give it away: it costs nothing on the quote but still prints
   on the event order and pack list.
4. Change **Agreed** to the negotiated rate on any line. The **Rack**
   column keeps the list price.
5. Optional: **Discount on the whole quote** and **Service charge %**.
6. Click **Save & reprice**.

![What they're buying: hall rental, menu, decor, DJ, stage, LED wall and valet as chargeable lines, and three complimentary lines, with the totals](/img/banquets/quote.png)

A discount on the whole quote spreads across the lines in proportion, so
each line keeps its own tax rate. Every change is recorded under **How
the price moved**, with what the quote was worth before and after. Things
still being agreed go under **Open items**, each with what agreeing it
would do to the price.

The **Margin** step shows revenue, cost and margin. Choose the menu's
dishes (or put a cost on the services in the catalogue) for the margin to
count them. Quotes below the floor margin are flagged.

## Send the quotation

1. Open the **Documents** step.
2. Click **Stamp quote v1**. This numbers the quotation and sets it valid
   for 15 days. Stamp again after a change to issue the next version.
3. Click **Email to guest** or **WhatsApp guest**. Email needs a customer
   email on the Enquiry step; WhatsApp needs a phone and a connected
   [WhatsApp](/whatsapp) number.
4. Click **Open** on any document to view or print it.

![The Documents step: quotation, contract, event order, pack list and invoice, and the Issue buttons](/img/banquets/documents.png)

If a quote goes 3 days with no reply, the function shows on the morning
list, and Kamra emails the guest a reminder when it has their email.

## Record the guest's answer and confirm

1. On the function, click **Guest response**.
2. Pick the **Outcome** (**Confirmed**, **Changes Requested**,
   **Declined**) and how you **Heard via** (phone, email, WhatsApp).
3. Leave **Also move the function to Confirmed** ticked to confirm it now.
4. Click **Save response**.

![The Guest response panel on a tentative function: outcome Confirmed, heard via Phone, with the box to also confirm the function](/img/banquets/guest-response.png)

You can also move a function with the buttons at the top: **Tentative**,
**Confirm**, **Cancelled**, **Lost**, and **Reopen** to bring a
tentative, cancelled or lost function back to an enquiry. Cancelled and
Lost ask why; the reason is kept for the pipeline report. On a confirmed
function, **Close out** opens **The night** step (see
[Close out the night](#close-out-the-night)).

When a function is confirmed, Kamra creates the **Department checklist**
— Sales, Finance, Housekeeping and F&B tasks with due dates counted back
from the event — and tells those teams.

## Take payments

1. Open the **Money** step.
2. Click **Use the usual schedule** to set the default milestones
   (advance at confirmation, an interim payment, the balance on
   completion), or **Add** your own. Click **Save terms**.
3. When money comes in, click **Record money in**. Enter the **Amount**,
   **What is it** (Advance, Payment, Security Deposit, Refund), **How**,
   a **Reference**, and which milestone it **Ticks off**.

![Payment terms: booking advance received, before the function and on completion pending](/img/banquets/money.png)

A percentage milestone follows the quote when it changes; a fixed amount
stays put. A security deposit is held money, not payment: it never comes
off the bill.

### Put the function on the group's bill

To put the function on the same bill as the guests' rooms, tie it to
their group booking:

1. In **Group booking** (on the **Enquiry** step's billing card, or
   on the **Money** step next to **Post to the bill**), pick the group.
2. Click **Tie to group**.
3. Once the function is confirmed, click **Post to the bill**. It posts
   to the group's master folio (see [Billing](/billing)).

![The Settlement card on the Money step: the Group booking list with the Sharma-Verma wedding guests picked, Tie to group, and Post to the bill](/img/banquets/group-link.png)

The list shows this hotel's groups, not cancelled, staying within a month
either side of the event. A group already tied to another function is
greyed out. Pick **Not tied to a group** and click **Untie** to undo it.
Once the function is billed or completed, its group can't change.

## Issue the event order

1. Confirm the function.
2. On **Documents**, click **Issue event order**.
3. Click **Open** on **Event order (BEO)** and **Print**.

The event order is the running sheet for the banquet, kitchen and AV
teams: venue, timings, pax, setup, every line (free ones too) with menus
expanded into courses, and the event order notes. The **Pack list** says
what has to reach the hall and by when.

![The banquet event order for the Sharma-Verma reception: function details, running order and what's included](/img/banquets/beo.png)

## Close out the night

1. After the function, open **The night**.
2. Click **Close out the function**.
3. Enter **Covers actually served**. The bill uses the guaranteed pax or
   the actual count, whichever is higher.
4. If something was broken, enter what to **Deduct from the deposit** and
   **What was damaged**.
5. Leave **Return the deposit balance now** ticked to refund the rest,
   and pick how.
6. Click **Close it out**.

![Close out the function: covers served, damages, deposit return and the amounts held and returning](/img/banquets/close-out.png)

The function becomes **Completed**. Damages are kept as a charge on the
function, and the refund is a line on its ledger.

### Reopen a closed function

A Revenue Manager, Finance or Hotel Admin user can reopen a
**Completed** function to correct the sheet:

1. Click **Reopen** at the top of the function.
2. Write **Why reopen it?** (required) and click **Reopen**.

The function goes back to **Confirmed**, and the reason goes on its notes
and the activity log. Receipts, refunds and damages already recorded stay
as they are. On **The night**, click **Close it again**: this time it only
re-counts the covers. Bill anything further as a line on the function.
A function already posted to a folio can't be reopened; correct the bill
on the folio instead.

## Print the registers

**Banquets & Groups → Registers**: pick the period once, then switch
between **Functions**, **Quotations**, **Enquiries**, **Cash book** and
**Sales summary**. Each has **CSV** and **Print**.

![The banquet registers for October: one function with pax, value, received and outstanding](/img/banquets/registers.png)

## Block rooms for a group

For the guests' rooms, use **Groups & Blocks**:

1. Click **New**, enter the **Group name**, **Arrival**, **Departure** and
   **Block cutoff**, and **Save**.
2. Open the group. Click **Add room type**, enter how many rooms, and
   **Save block**.
3. Click **Confirm block** when the guest agrees.
4. As names come in, use **Pick up a room** to book a guest into the
   block. Unsold blocked rooms go back on sale after the cutoff.

![A group: Sharma-Verma wedding guests, Open, with Add room type, Confirm block and Open group folio](/img/banquets/group.png)

To hold a changing room for the wedding party, use **Green room** on the
function's Enquiry step. It puts a real room block on that room, so it
leaves sellable inventory.

## Settings

**Banquets & Groups → Settings**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target margin | 40% | The margin quotes aim for. |
| Floor margin | 25% | Quotes below this are flagged for approval. |
| Advance at confirmation | 25% | First milestone on a new function. |
| Interim payment | 50% | Second milestone. |
| Interim due | 15 days before | When the interim falls due. |
| Follow up an enquiry after | 2 days | The follow-up date on a new enquiry. |

## Good to know

- **Needs chasing** on the Banquets home lists what each open function
  needs: follow-ups due, a quote with no reply for 3 days, tentative holds
  running out within 3 days, payments due, a confirmed function with no
  event order 7 days out, no guaranteed pax 3 days out, an unsigned
  contract or nothing received 14 days out, and functions that have passed
  but aren't closed.
- Every morning at 08:30 Kamra sends the urgent items for the next 45 days
  to the function's owner by WhatsApp when a number is connected, or to
  the Front Desk team.
- Confirming without a guaranteed pax uses the expected pax as the
  guarantee.
- Only a confirmed function gets an event order or can be closed out.
- A **Completed** function can't be edited until someone reopens it
  (see [Reopen a closed function](#reopen-a-closed-function)).
- The only way to **Completed** is the close-out on **The night**, so
  the covers and the deposit are always settled. The **Close out**
  button at the top takes you there.
- Alcohol never goes on a company or group bill. **Post to the bill**
  leaves those lines out, to be settled separately.

## Troubleshooting

**"… is already confirmed for … on …".** Another confirmed function holds
that hall for overlapping hours. Move one of them, or pick another hall.

**Email to guest is greyed out.** Stamp the quote first, and add the
customer's email on the Enquiry step.

**Post to the bill is greyed out.** The function isn't tied to a group
booking (pick one in **Group booking** and click **Tie to group**), or
it's still an enquiry.

**"Only a closed (Completed) function can be reopened …"** or **"This
function is already billed to …"** when reopening. Only a completed
function that isn't on a folio yet can be reopened.

**The margin looks too good.** The yellow note on the Margin step lists
the lines that have no cost yet. Choose the menu's dishes, or add a cost
to the service in the catalogue.

## For developers

Functions live in `kamra/banquet.py` and `kamra/banquet_ops.py` (the
`Venue Booking` doctype). AI agents use the `banquet_*` tools —
`banquet_availability`, `banquet_enquiry`, `banquet_add_menu`,
`banquet_quote`, `banquet_status`, `banquet_record_receipt`,
`banquet_link_group`, `banquet_close_out` and more. Reopening a closed
function is `kamra.banquet.reopen_function` (no MCP tool). See [MCP tools](/mcp-tools) and the
[API reference](/api-reference#banquets-functions-quotations-event-orders).
