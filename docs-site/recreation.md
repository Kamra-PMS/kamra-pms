# Recreation - gym, pool, spa and members

The **Recreation** app runs the gym, pool, spa, sauna, studio or court:
in-house guests come in free or on their bill, outside customers come in
on a membership or pay at the desk, and spa treatments and pool sessions
are booked so nothing is double-booked. Front Desk, F&B Manager and Hotel
Admin staff use it; Finance can read it.

## Before you start (Hotel Admin)

1. **Add each facility.** **Recreation → Add a facility or a plan**: the
   **Facility name**, the **Category** (Gym, Pool, Spa, ...) and the
   **Session price** an outside customer pays per visit. A **Spa** room
   is added with capacity 1, so it takes one treatment at a time. For a
   pool or court that is booked by the hour, enter **Session minutes**:
   the day is cut into sessions of that length between opening and
   closing.
2. **Add the plans** members buy: a **Plan name**, **Day / Week / Month /
   Year** and the price. Tick the facilities it opens; tick none and it
   opens every facility. A monthly plan bought on the 15th runs through
   the 14th; bought on 31 January, through the last day of February.
3. **Spa:** on **Spa and sessions**, add each **treatment** (name,
   minutes, price; it is booked in the facility picked above) and each
   **therapist**.

## Let someone in

On **Desk**, pick the facility, then who is coming in: a checked-in guest
or an outside customer.

- A **guest** comes in free (**Complimentary**) or on the folio at the
  session price.
- A **customer** with a membership that opens this facility comes in on
  it. A plan for this facility is used before an every-facility plan,
  and the one ending soonest first. A plan with a visit limit stops
  covering once it is used up.
- Otherwise the customer pays the session price. Pick how (**Cash**,
  **Card**, ...). The money goes in your till like any payment, and a
  **receipt** opens to print. It is a receipt, not a tax invoice.

Capacity and opening hours are checked every time.

**QR passes:** on **Members**, **Show pass** shows a customer's QR code;
the same works for a guest's stay. At the desk, scan it into **Scan a
pass** (a scanner types the code and presses Enter) instead of picking
the person. A pass that was not made by your Kamra is refused.

## Sell and renew memberships

On **Desk → Membership**, add the customer, pick the plan and how they
paid, and press **Sell membership**. The fee goes in the till with a
receipt. On **Members**, **Renew** sells the same plan again from the day
after the current one ends (or from today, if it has lapsed). Memberships
past their last day are marked Expired each night.

## Book a treatment or a session

On **Spa and sessions**, pick the day, the facility and who it is for.

- **Treatment:** pick the treatment (its room is picked for you) and the
  therapist, the time, and press **Book**. The treatment's minutes set
  the end. A room never takes two treatments at once, and a therapist is
  never booked twice at the same time.
- **Session:** a facility with session minutes shows each session and how
  many places are free; press one to book it. A full session can't be
  booked.

When they arrive, press **Arrived**: the booking becomes a visit and is
paid like one (a guest's treatment goes on the folio at the treatment's
price). **No show** and **Cancel** free the slot.

## Members and their ledger

**Members** shows everything one customer bought and used, oldest first:
each membership and renewal with its fee, each visit with the visits left
on a limited plan, what they paid, and the receipt for each payment.

## Report

**Report** covers a date range (this month by default): visits and
attendance by day, hotel guests against outside customers, active and
expired memberships and those ending in the next 7 days, revenue from
guests (on the folio), walk-in payments and membership fees, and visits
and revenue per facility.

## Good to know

- Recreation money from outside customers is an operational receipt in
  the till, not a room folio and not a tax invoice. A guest's paid access
  and treatments are on their bill.
- Registering a customer does not create a stay.
