# Kamra name and brand

The code is AGPL-3.0: you may use, change, host and sell it under that
license. The name "Kamra", "Kamra PMS", "Kamra Agent" and the Kamra mark
(see [`branding/`](branding/README.md)) are not part of that license. This
page says how to use them, in the same spirit as Frappe and ERPNext.

## Fine without asking

- Running Kamra for your own hotels, or for clients, and saying so:
  "runs on Kamra PMS", "Kamra implementation by Example Co".
- Putting a hotel's or a partner's own logo and name in Kamra's settings.
  Kamra shows them on screen, bills and emails next to its own name.
- Writing about Kamra, teaching it, and linking to it with the mark.
- Shipping the unmodified Kamra mark with an unmodified or patched release.

## Needs a different name

- A fork you distribute or offer as a service with changes we did not
  ship. Call it something else and say it is based on Kamra PMS.
- A product, company or domain name that contains "Kamra", or a logo that
  looks like the Kamra mark.
- Removing Kamra's name or mark from the app and presenting it as your
  own product. The AGPL lets you do that in your own fork; it does not let
  that fork use the Kamra name.

## Not allowed

- Implying that you are the Kamra project, or that we certify, endorse or
  support your build or service when we do not.

Implementing Kamra for hotels and want to be listed as a partner, or not
sure whether a use is fine? Open an issue or write to the maintainers
listed in [README.md](README.md).
